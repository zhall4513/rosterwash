# Business rules
RosterWash refers fleet operators to independent washing providers; it performs no washing. Default referral economics are 10% of eligible collected washing revenue for 24 months from first successful qualifying payment. No paid event currently starts protection automatically. Customer sharing requires consent. One provider gets an exclusive offer at once; expired/declined offers advance sequentially. Intake defaults closed and requires an active region. Partners require manual approval, reviewed insurance, connected Stripe and a signed agreement. Providers control quotes, schedule and service. Existing-customer exceptions, exact legal terms, business-hour/holiday policy and applicable law require review.
